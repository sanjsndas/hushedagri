    This Cookies Policy explains how Monument & Grain uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. We keep our use of cookies to an absolute minimum—only what is necessary for the site to function properly. We do not use cookies for advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking.

    Last updated: 1 March 2026 Version 1.1 Applies to: monumentandgrain.com

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by the site you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a different domain).

    Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

    2. How we use cookies

    Monument & Grain uses only essential cookies. These are strictly necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms.

    We do not use:

    • Advertising or targeting cookies
    • Third-party analytics cookies that track you across sites
    • Social media cookies that track your browsing activity
    • Any form of cross-site or cross-device tracking

    You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site may not work as a result.

    3. Cookies we use

    The table below lists the specific cookies we use on this site and their purpose.

    Name Type Purpose Duration
    mg_session Essential Maintains your session state as you navigate the site (e.g., remembering form inputs during a single visit). Session
    mg_consent Essential Records that you have seen our cookie notice and acknowledged this policy. 12 months
    mg_theme Essential Stores your display preference (light or dark mode) if you have set one. 12 months
    mg_search Essential Remembers your last search query within a session so you can refine it. Session

    We do not use any third-party cookies. All cookies listed above are first-party cookies set directly by monumentandgrain.com.

    4. Your cookie choices

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Because we only use essential cookies, there is no consent banner for advertising or tracking cookies—there are none to consent to. However, you can still manage cookies through your browser settings.

    Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

    Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of this site may become inaccessible or not function properly. For example, you may not be able to submit a contact form or your preferences may not be remembered.

    To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites (if we were to use it—we do not), visit Google Analytics Opt-out.

    6. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. Monument & Grain does not track you across sites, so DNT signals do not change how we operate. We do not respond to DNT signals because we do not engage in the tracking that DNT is designed to prevent.

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

    Your continued use of the site after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
