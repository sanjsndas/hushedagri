    • Privacy Policy

    Monument & Grain is an independent editorial publication. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency and care. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your personal data.

    Last updated: 1 March 2026 Version 1.1 Applies to: monumentandgrain.com

    1. Information we collect

    We collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to operate this publication and to serve our readers well. The categories of data we may collect include:

    • Contact information: If you choose to contact us via the form on our Contact page or by email, we receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.
    • Newsletter subscription: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address solely for the purpose of delivering the newsletter to you.
    • Anonymous analytics: We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how readers use the site—pages visited, time on page, referring sources. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.
    • Technical data: Like most websites, our servers automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and operating system. These logs are used only for security and diagnostic purposes and are retained for a short period.

    We do not collect sensitive personal data, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 16.

    2. How we use your information

    The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

    • To respond to your inquiries, pitches, corrections, or partnership proposals.
    • To send you our newsletter if you have opted in to receive it.
    • To understand how our content is being read so we can improve it.
    • To maintain the security and integrity of our website and systems.
    • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

    We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third party.

    3. Legal basis for processing

    Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

    • Consent: When you subscribe to our newsletter or voluntarily submit a contact form, you consent to us processing your data for that specific purpose.
    • Legitimate interests: We have a legitimate interest in understanding how our site is used and in maintaining its security, provided that this does not override your rights and interests.
    • Legal obligation: In rare cases, we may be required to process or retain data to comply with the law.

    4. Cookies and tracking

    This site uses essential cookies only—those required for basic functionality, such as remembering your navigation state or form inputs during a session. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or cross-site tracking technologies.

    If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored securely and used solely for the purpose of delivering our weekly dispatch. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

    For more detail, please see our Cookies Policy.

    5. Data retention

    We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

    • Contact form submissions and email correspondence: retained for up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for legal or editorial reasons.
    • Newsletter subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
    • Server logs and analytics data: retained for a maximum of 12 months in aggregated or anonymised form.

    When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

    6. Data sharing and disclosure

    We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

    • Service providers: We may use trusted third-party services for email delivery (newsletter) and website hosting. These providers process data only on our instructions and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
    • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
    • Protection of rights: We may disclose data where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Monument & Grain, our readers, or the public.

    Any third-party service providers we use are carefully selected and contractually bound to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Right of access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Right to erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
    • Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
    • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
    • Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
    • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@monumentandgrain.com. We will respond within 30 days.

    8. International transfers

    Our editorial team is based in the European Union and the United States. If you are located outside these regions, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries that may not have the same data protection laws as your own.

    Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data receives an adequate level of protection, including standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission where applicable.

    9. Security

    We take the security of your data seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

    These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular review of our data practices. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify newsletter subscribers directly.

    We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.

    11. Contact us

    If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our handling of your personal data, please contact us:

    Email: privacy@monumentandgrain.com
    Post: Monument & Grain, Editorial Office, 14 Rue des Archives, 75004 Paris, France

    If you are located in the European Union and believe we have not complied with applicable data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
