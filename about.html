    • About Monument & Grain

    An independent editorial publication about architecture, interior design, materials, and the quiet craft of space.

    Our Mission

    Monument & Grain exists to publish considered, long-form writing about the built environment—writing that treats architecture and interior design not as lifestyle trends but as disciplines worthy of serious attention. We are interested in the principles that make spaces endure: proportion, material honesty, light, restraint, and the relationship between human beings and the rooms they inhabit.

    In a media landscape saturated with listicles, product roundups, and shallow trend reporting, we aim to offer something different: depth over speed, principles over fashions, clarity over cleverness. We believe that good writing about space is as important as good building, and that both require patience, precision, and a willingness to think slowly.

    "Architecture is the thoughtful making of space." — Louis Kahn

    Our readers include practicing architects, interior designers, materials specialists, students, and discerning homeowners who care about how buildings are made and how they feel to live in. We write for people who read—carefully, slowly, and with attention.

    What We Cover

    Our editorial scope spans the full range of the built environment, from the scale of the city block to the detail of a door handle. Core subjects include:

    • Residential architecture — houses, apartments, and the design of domestic space
    • Interior design — layouts, finishes, furniture, and the craft of rooms
    • Materials — stone, timber, plaster, metal, and the ethics of honest construction
    • Lighting — natural and artificial, and the shaping of atmosphere
    • Small spaces — compact living, storage, and spatial generosity
    • Space planning — floor plans, circulation, and the arguments encoded in layout
    • Furniture and objects — the things we live with and how they are made

    We publish practical guides, critical essays, case studies, and interviews. Every article is written by someone with direct experience in the field—architects, designers, makers, and writers who have spent time with the materials and spaces they describe.

    Editorial Principles

    We hold ourselves to a small number of firm commitments. These principles guide everything we publish.

    Depth over speed

    We would rather publish one carefully considered article a week than five rushed ones a day. Our pieces take time to research, report, and edit.

    Principles over trends

    We are less interested in what is fashionable this season than in what has proven true over decades—and what is likely to remain true.

    Clarity over cleverness

    Good design writing should illuminate, not obscure. We aim for plain language, precise description, and arguments that hold up to scrutiny.

    Independence

    We are reader-supported and editorially independent. We do not publish sponsored content disguised as editorial, and we disclose all relationships.

    How We Work

    Monument & Grain is published independently. We are not owned by a media conglomerate, an advertising agency, or a furniture brand. Our revenue comes from reader subscriptions and from carefully selected partnerships that align with our editorial values.

    We do not accept payment for coverage. We do not publish advertorials. When we partner with a brand or institution—for a sponsored series, for example—we label it clearly and maintain full editorial control over the content. Our readers' trust is the only asset we have, and we protect it carefully.

    If you are interested in working with us, please see our contact page.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who writes for Monument & Grain?

    +

    Our articles are written by practicing architects, interior designers, materials specialists, and experienced design journalists. Every contributor has direct experience in the field they write about. We do not use AI to generate editorial content.

    How often do you publish?

    +

    We publish two to three new articles each week, plus a weekly newsletter. Long-form guides and critical essays appear roughly once a month. Quality and depth matter more to us than volume.

    Can I pitch an article?

    +

    Yes. We welcome thoughtful pitches from writers and practitioners. Please send a brief outline (200–300 words) along with relevant writing samples to editors@monumentandgrain.com. We read every pitch, though we cannot always respond individually.

    Do you accept sponsored content?

    +

    We occasionally work with brands and institutions on clearly labeled sponsored series, but we do not publish advertorials disguised as editorial. All partnerships are disclosed at the top of the article, and we retain full editorial control over the content.

    How can I support the publication?

    +

    The best way to support us is to subscribe to our newsletter and share our articles with others who might find them useful. We also offer a paid membership with access to our full archive and occasional member-only essays.
