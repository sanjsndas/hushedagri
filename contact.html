    Editorial

    Pitches & Submissions

    We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays on architecture, interiors, and material culture. If you have a story that fits our editorial stance—depth over speed, principles over trends—we would like to hear from you.

    editors@monumentandgrain.com

    Please include a brief outline and relevant writing samples. Response time: 5–10 business days.

    Partnerships

    Advertising & Collaborations

    We work with a small number of partners whose values align with our editorial mission. If you are interested in sponsoring a series, collaborating on a project, or advertising in our newsletter, please get in touch.

    partners@monumentandgrain.com

    We review partnership inquiries on a rolling basis.

    Corrections

    Accuracy & Feedback

    We take accuracy seriously. If you have spotted an error in one of our articles, or if you have context that would improve a piece, please let us know. We are committed to transparency and will publish corrections where warranted.

    corrections@monumentandgrain.com

    Please include the article title and a link where possible.

    From the Editors

    Monument & Grain is an independent publication. We are not affiliated with any architectural practice, design studio, or commercial interest. Our editorial decisions are made solely on the basis of quality, relevance, and alignment with our mission: to publish considered writing about the built environment.
